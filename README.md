# [Bon Appétit](https://www.modrinth.com/mod/bon-appetit)
Bon Appétit is a mod designed to be an alternative to popular food mods like Farmer’s Delight in order to add more diversity and strategy to food.

The flavors in the mod do not give their effects by default; rather, you have to refine them into proper meals. The more complex the meal, the longer and stronger the effect, giving a reason to experiment with different food combinations.
